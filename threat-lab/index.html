Next steps
  1. Call 911 now. Nothing on this site replaces emergency services.
  2. Use a device the other person can't access. Update it, change passwords from that device, turn on two-factor sign-in, and review location sharing and unknown apps.
  3. Write down what happened, when, and who was involved. Save screenshots, messages and photos, and store copies offline (for example, a USB drive kept somewhere safe).
  4. Reach us through Signal or encrypted email above. Share details only there.
  5. We reply as capacity allows, help assess the situation and plan, and refer you to law enforcement, legal aid or advocates where appropriate. We can't promise outcomes.
