Research areas

1. Coastal energy access

Field study of public EV charging availability, pricing, and reliability on public coastal land in San Diego, supported by California Public Records Act requests.

2. Off-grid energy resilience

Measuring real-world battery capacity, solar, and charging performance in mobile and off-grid living systems.

3. Critical-mineral market integrity

Tracking copper and scrap-metal pricing against global benchmarks to expose fraud in materials essential to the energy transition.
