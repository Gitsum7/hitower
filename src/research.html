Research directions

Led by Principal Investigator Jack Knight. Primary field: Earth & Environmental Sciences. Established 2026; methods and findings will be published here as they are completed.

1. Natural-hazard & severe-weather forecasting

Localized storm, flood and fire-weather forecasting and alerting for under-served and off-grid communities, built on public meteorological and hazard data. See the Disaster Forecast on the home page.

2. Geospatial field & coastal-access studies

Field mapping of trails and public lands, and a study of public access on San Diego coastal land informed by California Public Records Act requests.

3. Emergency management & public safety

How open, public-source information can support disaster response and community safety, including documented methods for verifying public hazard reports.
