Report safely

This page collects no data. Nothing you do here is sent to HiTower.

Immediate danger

Call 911

Lines
  • National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233 · thehotline.org
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988 · 988lifeline.org
  • FBI tips: tips.fbi.gov
Private channels
  • Signal: end-to-end encrypted messaging.
  • Proton: encrypted email.
  • SecureDrop: how newsrooms accept anonymous tips.
Safety planning
  • Use a device the other person cannot access, and clear your browser history if needed.
  • Keep copies of IDs, documents and evidence somewhere safe outside the home.
  • Choose a code word with someone you trust that means "call for help."
  • Plan where you would go and how you would get there.
  • Advocates at the hotlines above can help build a plan for your situation.
