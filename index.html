The mission

Systems fail quietly, then all at once. Power cuts out, water runs short, help arrives late, and the people at the edges are reached last. HiTower is a small, independent lab run by a team of patriots. We design self-reliant power, water and safety systems that are cheap to build, simple to repair and open for anyone to copy.

I // Pillar

Adaptive Adventure Programs

Accessible off-grid expeditions and adapted gear that make the wild reachable for people with disabilities.

II // Pillar

Hydrogen & Off-Grid Power

Fuel cells and hybrid battery systems for van conversions and portable generators.

III // Pillar

Self-Generating Water & Power

Resilient water and power for off-grid and underserved communities, including safety and disaster-preparedness engineering for underserved Christian communities worldwide.

IV // Pillar

Open Hardware & Plain-Text Software

Designs and code anyone can read, audit and run. Self-reliance without lock-in.
