Directive

The Mission

Systems fail quietly, then all at once. Power cuts out, water runs short, help arrives late, and the people at the edges are always the last to be reached. HiTower is a small, independent lab built by a team of patriots to change that: we design self-reliant power, water, and safety systems that are cheap enough to build, simple enough to repair, and open enough for anyone to copy.

Four Pillars

The Work

I

Adaptive Adventure

Adventure programs for people with disabilities: accessible off-grid expeditions, adapted gear, and field-tested logistics that make the wild reachable for everyone.

II

Hydrogen & Off-Grid Power

Hydrogen fuel cells and hybrid battery systems for van conversions and portable generators. We measure real-world loads and build power that holds when the grid doesn't.

III

Water & Power Sanctuaries

Self-generating water and power for off-grid and underserved communities, including resilience, safety, and disaster-preparedness engineering for underserved Christian communities around the world.

IV

Open Hardware, Plain Text

Open hardware designs and plain-text software anyone can read, audit, and run. Cost-effective tools for self-reliant communities, free from lock-in.

Creed

Why the Tower

A watchman on a high tower sees the storm before it lands. That's our posture: look further, prepare earlier, and build things that protect the soul and the body of the communities we serve. We publish what we learn, we open-source what we build, and we answer to the work.
