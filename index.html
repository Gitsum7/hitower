The mission

Hazards arrive quietly, then all at once. Storms, floods, wildfire weather and coastal events reach the people at the edges first and help reaches them last. HiTower Institute is a nonprofit research institute in formation, led by Principal Investigator Jack Knight. We study how forecasts, maps and public information can reach those communities sooner, and we publish our methods openly.

I // Research direction

Natural-Hazard & Severe-Weather Forecasting

Earth & environmental science: localized storm, flood and fire-weather forecasting and alerting from public meteorological and hazard data, including the Disaster Forecast above.

II // Research direction

Geospatial Field & Coastal-Access Studies

Field mapping of trails and public lands, and a study of public access on San Diego coastal land informed by California Public Records Act requests.

III // Research direction

Emergency Management & Public Safety

How open, public-source information can support disaster response and community safety, including methods for verifying public hazard reports.

IV // Program

Adaptive Adventure Programs

Accessible field expeditions and adapted gear that make the outdoors reachable for people with disabilities.

Why the tower

A watchman on a high tower sees the storm before it lands. Look further, warn earlier, and serve the overlooked.
