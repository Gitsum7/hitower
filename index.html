HITOWER

An independent institute for coastal resilience and energy science

HomeResearchTeamPrincipal Investigator

HiTower takes its name and spirit from the WWII-era tradition of small, quiet research groups that worked on hard problems from a high vantage point. We bring that "view from the tower" to modern Earth and environmental science.

Mission

Measure, model, and publish open findings on how coastal communities and people living off-grid can stay powered and resilient as the climate changes.

Status

Founded in 2026 as an independent research project, working toward nonprofit incorporation.
