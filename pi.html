Summary

Jack Knight founded HiTower to do hands-on, public-interest research from the field. He lives and works off-grid, and his research grows out of that life: what it really takes to keep power, water and access flowing for people outside the usual systems.

01 // Line of inquiry

Coastal energy access

A California Public Records Act study of public EV charging access and pricing on public coastal land.

02 // Line of inquiry

Off-grid power resilience

Logging and modeling battery capacity and real-world energy use in mobile living.

03 // Line of inquiry

Hydrogen for mobile systems

Fuel cells and hybrid systems for van conversions and portable generators.

04 // Line of inquiry

Critical-mineral market integrity

Detecting fraudulent bulk copper and scrap offers on B2B marketplaces against benchmark pricing.
