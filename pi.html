Jack Knight

Founder & Principal Investigator

An independent researcher based in San Diego, California, Jack studies coastal energy access, off-grid power systems, and market integrity in critical minerals. He works hands-on from a mobile field lab and has firsthand experience with the infrastructure he studies.

Current projects

  • Coastal EV charging access and pricing (CPRA records study)
  • Off-grid battery capacity and resilience measurement
  • Copper and scrap-metal fraud detection
